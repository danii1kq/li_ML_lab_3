After running all the models, I would pick the degree-2 polynomial regression on mass_kg, it gave the best cross-validation score (R² = 0.355). The simple linear model was fine too: y = 19.647 + -0.007 * x, with test R² = 0.602 against train R² = 0.720, so it works on new data, not just the training set, but cross-validation score was low (0.313 ± 0.539), which shows some folds were hard to predict. Adding features helped only a bit (0.332 with power_hp, 0.326 with all five), so more features is not always better. Past degree 2 the score dropped (0.355 → 0.337) model started to overfit. Thats why I chose degree 2, its simple and stable
